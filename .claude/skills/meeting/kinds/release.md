# Release

What was released, and the milestones that come next. Held only when no decided milestone is left, so it plans ahead.

## Agenda

1. **Released:** the version, its features against the entry conditions, open findings.
2. **Next milestones:** the next two or three of the roadmap (fewer if later ones cannot be judged yet), each with its features as entry conditions, each with a *done when*, and its budget.
3. **Features:** for every milestone planned, per feature the facts and options of `feature.md` (Preparation, Agenda 1–3), and the verdict: go, smaller scope or no-go.

## Outcome

The first planned milestone active in `timeline.md`, the others under *Planned milestones* marked `decided (meeting #n)` with their entry conditions, and every budget in the Overview; for each go the outcome of `feature.md`; the GitHub milestones. The shift then builds and releases them one after another without asking again.
