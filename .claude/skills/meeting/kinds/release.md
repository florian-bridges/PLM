# Release

What a release contained, and what comes next.

## Agenda

1. **Released:** the version, its features against the entry conditions, open findings.
2. **Next milestone:** its features as entry conditions, each with a *done when*, and its budget.
3. **Features:** per feature the facts and options of `feature.md` (Preparation, Agenda 1–3), and the verdict: go, smaller scope or no-go.

## Outcome

The next milestone active in `timeline.md`; for each go the outcome of `feature.md`; the GitHub milestone. The shift then builds without asking again.
