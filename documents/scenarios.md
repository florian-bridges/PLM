# User Scenarios

**US-1 — Requirements engineer**
Writes requirement documents from aircraft to equipment level, traced top to bottom, and exchanges them with customers and suppliers. (CR-04, CR-06, CR-07, CR-16)

**US-2 — Structure engineer**
Breaks the aircraft into assemblies and subassemblies, attaches CAD models, releases changes by merge and searches for items. (CR-02, CR-03, CR-05, CR-12)

**US-3 — Avionics engineer**
Defines custom electronics, COTS components with their documentation, and wiring; manages interfaces to the structure. (CR-01, CR-03, CR-04)

**US-4 — Software engineer**
Develops software for avionics components in separate repositories, traced to requirements. (CR-01, CR-06, CR-08)

**US-5 — Verification engineer**
Defines test cases for systems under test and records executed runs with their artifacts. (CR-09, CR-19)

**US-6 — Airworthiness engineer**
Reviews test coverage, traceability, suspect links and compliance across releases without local tools. (CR-09, CR-10, CR-11, CR-13)

**US-7 — Configuration manager**
Assigns item owners, assesses the impact of change requests, approves and merges changes with a recorded rationale and controls which partners may access which data. (CR-11, CR-13, CR-14, CR-15, CR-17, CR-18)
